        WHY BEYOND WORDS

        Make communication more accessible, one reviewed step at a time.

        Spoken or written English alone does not meet every communication need. We are exploring how editable text, visible meaning and an articulated avatar can help people discover signed communication.

        Sign Studio demonstrates the workflow. It is not a substitute for a qualified interpreter, an ASL course, or a validated conversation translator.

        ABOUT SIGN STUDIO

        Meet Aarav.

        The original project targets American Sign Language (ASL), not Indian Sign Language. Validated mode is the default and currently has zero approved clips. The live motion library shows current asset counts, source attribution and review status. Newly imported recordings remain unapproved. Technically checked source-word playback requires explicit expanded research consent; default validated translation stays restricted. Demo mode preserves six illustrative procedural motions. Missing terms never receive invented signs or fingerspelling.

        Aarav's retained MIT-licensed Rocketbox human model has articulated shoulders, elbows, wrists, fingers, and facial controls. Interface and speech language choices change appearance and labels; the signed language stays ASL. An ordered lexical queue is a prototype, not full grammatical translation.

        We studied Rylo's public workflow and published sources. No Rylo sign database, private model, generated video, or application code was imported. Recorded research motions come from the separate CC BY 4.0 3D-LEX archive; their sources and review status appear in the library.

        Multi-sentence input and 500+ words are supported. Limits are 10,000 words and 100,000 characters with explicit errors. Playback includes play, pause, resume, replay, stop, speed, progress, and coverage. Microphone transcripts stay editable before processing.

        Speech uses available browser voices with selection, rate, volume, mute, and stop. Natural voice availability depends on installed or browser-provided voices. Recognition and remote voices may use their providers' services. No API key or paid service is required.

        Camera input detects seven general poses, not continuous ASL. Five mappings are illustrative responses; two are optional player controls. Stable captures require your confirmation. Video stays on this device. Only confirmed illustration labels and scores enter local history; player controls stay in the browser. History can be exported or cleared.
