## Human-review acceptance evidence

For each exact utterance, record a fluent ASL signer or qualified interpreter's identity/qualification, date, intended meaning and dialect/variant; immutable source/runtime motion hashes; the chosen realization; handshape, palm orientation, location, movement and two-hand coordination; required gaze/facial/body expression; participant direction; transition/contact findings; rendering/framing findings on desktop and mobile; permitted English aliases and prohibited senses; and an explicit decision. Inspect source and Aarav render together. Technical playback success, general lexical reference agreement, and a neutral face are insufficient evidence of grammatical phrase approval.

Negation, speaker/addressee changes, sarcasm, names, urgency and question-versus-statement force must not be silently discarded. Fingerspelling is unavailable until valid assets and transitions are present. Existing English pattern forms in these files are proposed review scope only; no general sentence grammar has been added.
